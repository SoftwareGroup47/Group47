Code of Ethics

1. Fairness and Impartiality

We are committed to maintaining fairness and impartiality in the TA recruitment process, ensuring equal opportunities for all applicants:

  • No discrimination against any applicant based on gender, race, religion, age, or other personal characteristics
  • Evaluation based on applicants' skills, academic performance, and capabilities
  • Ensuring a transparent and open recruitment process

2. Integrity Principles

We require all users to adhere to principles of integrity when using the system:

  • Provide truthful and accurate personal information
  • Do not forge or tamper with application materials
  • Respect the intellectual property rights of others

3. Responsibility

We require all users to assume corresponding responsibilities when using the system:

  • MO users should carefully review applications and ensure reasonable recruitment decisions
  • TA users should diligently complete application information and ensure its accuracy
  • Administrators should manage the system impartially and ensure its proper operation

4. Privacy Protection

We value user privacy and commit to:

  • Protecting users' personal information
  • Not misusing user data
  • Complying with the privacy policy

5. Professionalism

We encourage all users to maintain professionalism:

  • MO users should professionally post job information with clear requirements
  • TA users should professionally complete application materials and showcase their abilities
  • All users should respect others and maintain professional communication

6. Compliance

We require system usage to comply with relevant laws and regulations:

  • Comply with data protection regulations
  • Comply with intellectual property regulations
  • Comply with education-related regulations
